# Signal Maze version 12: round entry shaft and varied TV sizes

Status: applied in Map Creation (`126262358232325`) on November 18, 2026, and copied into the game place as `ServerStorage.Maps.SignalMaze` (its `Terrain` region kept). `Workspace.SignalMaze` has `BuildVersion` 12. Save the Map Creation place to keep it.

Two edit-mode recipes, run in this order. Each returns a function, so load it as a temporary ModuleScript and call it.

## `entry-shaft-v12.luau`: round shaft

Replaces the square version 11 shaft ([ENTRY-SHAFT-V11.md](ENTRY-SHAFT-V11.md)). It rebuilds from the original ceiling saved in `ServerStorage.SignalMaze_V11Backup`, so it can be rerun.

- The ceiling is split around a 20 by 20 stud cut centred on `(147, 200)`. The nine `RoomFill_*` attachments keep their world positions.
- `EntryShaft.Collar`: 24 ceiling-material segments fill the cut down to a circular opening of radius 6.5.
- `EntryShaft.Lining`: three rows of nine bone-rigged flesh column meshes (tagged `SignalMazeFlesh`, so they breathe) form a round tube from the ceiling up to y 134, each row offset by half a column.
- `EntryShaft.Backing`: a dark 20-sided flesh wall behind the columns so no gaps show.
- `Cap`, three `Screens` TVs, three red `Glow` lights, and the `LevelEntry` and `ShaftTop` markers, as in version 11.
- Rim lumps around the opening were tried and left out: the straight column meshes made the opening read as a hexagon.

## `tv-sizes-v12.luau`: TV sizes

Version 7 gave nearly every TV an 8 to 12 stud housing. This pass rescales each wall TV with `Model:ScaleTo`, keeping its housing bottom height and glass front plane, so it grows up and out of the wall instead of into the floor.

- Target sizes are drawn from four classes: small (0.5 to 0.75 times, 32%), medium (0.85 to 1.05, 33%), large (1.25 to 1.6, 25%) and giant (1.9 to 2.5, 10%).
- TVs are placed largest first. A TV that is too wide (over 28 studs), too close to the ceiling, overhangs the end of its wall (rays from its edges must hit the `Collision` walls), runs into a side wall, or overlaps another TV shrinks by 12% steps; if nothing fits down to 0.45 times it keeps its old size.
- TVs that grow are pushed up to 1.4 studs further out of the wall so the larger glass stays clear of the flesh.
- Screen lights are recomputed: brightness scales with the square root of the size change, range is 9 plus 2.2 per stud of screen width (14 to 36 for red, 12 to 28 for static). The `MonsterSpawn` attachment is put back 2.5 studs in front of the glass, and `TvScale`, `ScreenSize` and a new `SizeClass` attribute are updated.
- Every TV records `BasePivot`, `BaseScale` and `BaseTvScale` (and its light `BaseLightBrightness`/`BaseLightRange`) the first time, and later runs start from those, so the pass can be rerun with a new seed.
- Result: 116 small, 211 medium, 29 large and 7 giant TVs, 2.5 to 22 studs wide (median 8). 13 kept their old size. The entry shaft TVs are left alone.

To undo the sizes, `ScaleTo` each TV back to its `BaseScale` and `PivotTo` its `BasePivot`.
